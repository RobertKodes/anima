Cookie Policy

Last updated: 31 August 2026 · ePrivacy / GDPR aligned

This policy explains how this website uses cookies and similar technologies (local storage).

What we use

  • Essential local storage — anima-cookie-consent (your banner choice), anima-scroll-mode (section scroll vs standard scroll). Required for remembered preferences. Legal basis: consent or legitimate interest for strictly necessary functionality.
  • No analytics cookies by default. We do not load Google Analytics, Meta Pixel, or similar unless we explicitly add them and you opt in.

Third-party resources

  • Google Fonts — may cause your browser to request font files from Google servers (IP address processed). You can block this with browser extensions or self-host fonts in a fork.
  • jsDelivr CDN — Three.js library for the 3D hero; CDN may log technical data.
  • GitHub Pages — hosting infrastructure.

Your choices

Use the cookie banner on first visit: “Essential only” stores only preference keys. “Accept all” currently enables the same essential storage only (no extra trackers at this time).

Clear site data in your browser to reset choices. Toggle “Standard scroll” to disable section scrolljacking (accessibility).

Duration

Local storage persists until you delete it or we change keys in a site update.

See our Privacy Policy for broader data protection information.
