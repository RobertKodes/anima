Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 August 2026 · Version 1.0

This Privacy Policy explains how we process personal data when you visit this presentation website or use the Anima open-source software. We aim to comply with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), UK GDPR, and other applicable privacy laws including the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA) where relevant.

1. Controller

The controller for this website is the Anima open-source project, represented by its maintainers. Contact: see Imprint or open an issue on GitHub.

2. Scope

  • This website (static pages hosted on GitHub Pages)
  • Anima software (runs locally on your device; we do not receive your chat or Sibyl data unless you choose to share it)

3. Data we process on this website

Data Purpose Legal basis (GDPR) Retention
Local storage (consent, scroll mode) Remember your cookie and accessibility choices Art. 6(1)(a) consent / (f) legitimate interest for essential prefs Until you clear browser storage
Server logs (GitHub Pages / CDN) Security, abuse prevention, technical delivery Art. 6(1)(f) legitimate interest Per GitHub/Microsoft retention policies

We do not sell personal information. We do not use non-essential cookies without consent.

4. Anima software (local)

Anima stores identity and memory in a local Sibyl database on your machine (default ~/.anima/). That data is not transmitted to project maintainers by default. If you configure cloud brains, your prompts are sent to the provider you choose under their terms.

5. Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict, port, or object to processing, and to withdraw consent. California residents may have rights to know, delete, and opt out of sale/sharing (we do not sell data).

To exercise rights regarding this website, contact us via GitHub. For data processed by GitHub or Google, refer to their policies.

You may lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority (EU/EEA) or the ICO (UK).

6. International transfers

GitHub Pages and CDN providers may process data in the United States. Where required, transfers rely on appropriate safeguards (e.g. EU-US Data Privacy Framework participants or Standard Contractual Clauses).

7. Children

This site and software are not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect children's data.

8. Changes

We will update this page when practices change. Material changes will be reflected in the “Last updated” date.
