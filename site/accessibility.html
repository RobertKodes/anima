Accessibility Statement

Last updated: 31 August 2026 · WCAG 2.1 Level AA target

We want this presentation site to be usable by as many people as possible, in line with the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA and applicable laws (e.g. EU Web Accessibility Directive, ADA Title III where relevant to public accommodations online).

Measures taken

  • Semantic HTML landmarks (header, main, nav, footer)
  • Skip link to main content
  • Visible focus indicators on interactive elements
  • Keyboard navigation for in-page anchor links
  • Native document scroll with optional GSAP scroll reveals
  • prefers-reduced-motion — disables GSAP animations, 3D motion, bento tilt, and magnetic buttons automatically
  • Alternative text / labels for the 3D canvas
  • Sufficient color contrast on primary text and controls (dark theme, amber accent on slate backgrounds)

Known limitations

  • Scroll-driven animations may be distracting; enable reduced motion in your OS settings to disable them.
  • 3D WebGL content may not be perceivable to all users; informational content is duplicated in text.
  • Bento card tilt and magnetic buttons require a pointer device; touch users get static cards.

Feedback

If you encounter barriers, please open an issue at github.com/RobertKodes/anima/issues with “a11y” in the title. We aim to respond within 14 days.

Enforcement procedure (EU)

In Romania, the relevant enforcement body for accessibility of websites can be contacted through national consumer and digital authorities. You may also contact us first so we can resolve issues promptly.
